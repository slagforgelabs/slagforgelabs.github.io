YOUR AGENT. YOUR MACHINE.

A Slagbot runs where you can see it. We size the machine to the work, or point it at rented inference when that makes more sense. Either way the bot, its memory, and its files stay with you.

Hardware

A Slagbot Remote needs very little. A small desktop machine is enough. When the work grows, or when nothing may leave the building, we spec and build a dedicated machine and install the whole system on site. Configurations are scoped per engagement and kept confidential.

Rented Or Local

Rented inference gives you a capable model without buying serious hardware. It suits steady work and quick starts, and it costs nothing up front. Local inference puts the models on your own machine, so nothing leaves your building unless you say so, with no per token invoice and no third party in the loop. Most clients run both lanes: local for sensitive work, rented for burst.

Slagboss

A Slagboss is a lead agent that runs a crew of smaller local Slagbots. It assigns the work, checks what comes back, and reports to one owner. You get one point of contact and a whole team behind it, all on your own hardware.

After Delivery

Build, install, train your people, support the system. Support terms are set per engagement. We have been building machines since the late nineties, gaming rigs first, then workstations and servers. A hardware shop that learned to train models, not a software company that bought a processor.
