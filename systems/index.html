INFERENCE INFRASTRUCTURE. OWNED, NOT RENTED.

Slagforge builds AI systems that run where the work happens. Two routes, one standard. Hosted integration for lean budgets. Dedicated local inference for work that cannot leave the building. Most clients land between the two, and we price to the order, not to a menu.

Hosted Integration

Plug into frontier models through a hosted API. We wire the fallback chain, cap the spend, and keep it running. No capital outlay. No rack. The right call for teams that need capability now and volume they can meter.

Dedicated Inference

For work that cannot leave the building, or volume that makes hosted billing look silly. We specify, source, build, and commission the box. You own the hardware. Traffic stays inside your walls. No per-token invoice and no third party in the loop. Configurations are scoped per engagement and kept confidential.

The Hybrid

Hosted for burst, local for sensitive. One system, two lanes. Priced to the order because the order decides the mix.

Established Stack

We run this stack in production. Local inference, quantized models, retrieval pipelines, and agent tooling. This is not a pilot program and it is not a proof of concept. We have been building machines since the late nineties, gaming rigs first, then workstations and servers. A hardware shop that learned to train models, not a software company that bought a GPU.
