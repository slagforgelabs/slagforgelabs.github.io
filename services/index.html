Scanning and prototyping

We can scan just about anything with photogrammetry, from a worn machine part to a whole room. Our crew synthesizes the data into whatever you need: measurements, a reverse engineered part, a print ready model, or a plain report. Then our in house 3D printers, run by the bots, turn it into a prototype you can hold. For industrial, engineering, and creative projects.
